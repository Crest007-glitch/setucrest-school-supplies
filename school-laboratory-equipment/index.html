FAQs

School laboratory-equipment sourcing questions.

Can you supply school laboratory equipment outside India?

International enquiries are welcome. Feasibility depends on the products, quantities, destination, shipping economics, applicable regulations and documentation requirements.

Can I send a complete laboratory BOQ?

Yes. A BOQ or subject-wise item list is the best starting point. Include specifications, quantities and your delivery destination so the requirement can be reviewed accurately.

Do you cover chemistry, physics and biology laboratories?

Yes. You can submit a combined or subject-specific requirement for chemistry, physics, biology or general-science laboratory equipment.

Do you accept small and medium-quantity enquiries?

Yes. Small, medium and bulk requirements can be discussed. Availability, minimum order quantity and delivery economics vary by product and destination.

Can you supply laboratory chemicals?

Chemical enquiries require separate review because availability, transport and regulatory requirements depend on the chemical and destination. Send the exact name, grade, concentration, pack size and quantity for an initial feasibility check.
