What to share for a furniture quotation.

Dimensions, quantity and delivery details are especially useful when the requirement is for a new room, expansion or complete campus setup.

Space and dimensionsShare room size, layout, reference photos or required furniture dimensions.
Quantity and useTell us the number of students, rooms, departments or furniture units required.

Specify furniture for the space and users

Share a room sketch or photographs with aisle, doorway and staircase measurements where relevant. Final dimensions, supply scope and delivery timing are confirmed in the quotation.

Quantity and delivery planning

Share the quantity for each specification, acceptable alternatives and your preferred receiving date. Minimum order quantities and delivery feasibility vary by item and are confirmed with the quotation. The written supply details should identify the agreed items, packing, delivery location and any assembly or fitting included.
