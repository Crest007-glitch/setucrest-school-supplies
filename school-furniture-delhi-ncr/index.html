What your institution can request.

01

Student Desks and Benches

Choose a school desk or bench and desk set around the pupils who will use it. Include the class group, preferred single-seat or shared layout, and the usable classroom dimensions. For classroom benches, specify the number of pupils per unit instead of giving only a total seat count.

  • Desk and seat heights appropriate to the age group
  • Top dimensions, frame material and finish
  • Existing furniture measurements when matching additions

02

Teacher Tables and Chairs

A teacher table needs space for lesson materials without narrowing the teaching area. Specify the tabletop dimensions, drawer or cupboard needs, and the chair type. Staff-room tables and office seating can be included in the same requirement list.

  • Table size, storage compartments and locks
  • Chair seat height and back support preference
  • Room access and assembly requirements

03

Classroom Storage and Furniture

Plan cupboards, shelves and learning-area furniture around what each room stores. State external dimensions, shelf configuration and locking requirements. Check that doors can open fully and that storage leaves clear circulation space.

  • Classroom cupboards and resource shelves
  • Library and reading-area tables and seating
  • Laboratory stools and tables to the specified use
