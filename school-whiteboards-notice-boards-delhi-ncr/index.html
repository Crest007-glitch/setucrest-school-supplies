Measurement checklist

What to share before quotation.

For multiple rooms, use one row per board so sizes and quantities remain clear.

Dimensions and quantityState width × height, measurement unit and number of boards.
Surface and frameSpecify writing/display surface, frame material and covered or open format.
Placement and deliveryShare wall/mobile preference, floor access and school delivery location.

Choose the board surface and format

Measure the usable wall area and record width × height with units. If you request a 120 × 90 cm board, state whether those measurements include the frame; this is a specification example, not a stock-size promise. Keep one line per size and room.

RequirementWhat to specifyWhat to confirm
WhiteboardDry-erase writing; state whether magnets must hold on the surface.Ask for the exact surface material, recommended markers and cleaning instructions.
