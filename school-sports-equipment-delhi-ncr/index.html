Quote accurately

What to include in your sports list.

Product names alone may not define quality or intended use. These details make comparison more meaningful.

Sport and age groupState the sport, classes or age group and whether products are for practice or competition.
Quantity and qualityGive unit quantities, preferred brands and acceptable quality level or alternatives.
Delivery and timelineShare the institution location and date by which the equipment is needed.

Specify sports equipment by activity and age group

Use one line for each size or specification. Where a school requires an approved brand or a particular standard, include that requirement with the item rather than assuming all options are interchangeable.

Specification checklist
RequirementWhat to specifyWhat to confirm
Balls and gamesSport, size, material and intended surface.Separate training, recreational and competition requirements.
Nets and accessoriesDimensions, fixing arrangement and indoor/outdoor use.Include posts, ropes or other accessories only when required.
Physical educationActivity, age group, participant count and quantity per item.Specify sizes and colours when equipment is allocated by group.
Storage and supplyStorage space, pack quantities and delivery location.Confirm how mixed items should be labelled and grouped.

Quantity and delivery planning

Share the quantity for each specification, acceptable alternatives and your preferred receiving date. Minimum order quantities and delivery feasibility vary by item and are confirmed with the quotation. The written supply details should identify the agreed items, packing, delivery location and any assembly or fitting included.
