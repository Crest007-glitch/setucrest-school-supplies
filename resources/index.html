Before you send your list

  1. Check that every quantity has a unit, such as pieces, sets or packs.
  2. Identify required specifications and where alternatives are acceptable.
  3. Add the school address, receiving contact, preferred date and access details.
  4. State whether fitting, assembly or separate department packing is needed.

Minimum quantities, product options and delivery arrangements are confirmed in the quotation. A preferred date helps planning and is not a delivery commitment.
