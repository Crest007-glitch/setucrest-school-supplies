FAQs

Questions institutions usually ask.

If your question is not listed, send your requirement and we will clarify the next step.

What types of products can I request?

You can enquire about classroom and office essentials, laboratory equipment, art and craft supplies, sports equipment, furniture and classroom requirements, hygiene products, and printing or customised supplies.

Do you supply only in bulk?

We discuss trial, smaller, bulk, annual and recurring requirements depending on the product, specification and supplier capability. Share the quantity you need for confirmation.

How do I receive a quotation?

Send the product details, specifications, quantity, delivery location and preferred timeline. We review the requirement and respond with suitable options and quotation details.

Can you arrange customised or branded items?

Yes, you can share your artwork, branding, dimensions, material or other specifications. We will review the requirement and confirm what can be arranged.

Which locations do you cover?

Our current service focus is Delhi-NCR, including Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Faridabad. Share your exact delivery location when you enquire.
